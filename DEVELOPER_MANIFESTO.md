# Developer Manifesto

I believe software should solve real problems.

I believe understanding is more valuable than memorization.

I believe AI should accelerate thinking, not replace it.

I believe documentation is part of engineering.

I believe clean, readable code is an act of respect for future developers.

I believe product decisions should begin with the people using the software.

I believe testing is part of building, not something added at the end.

I believe good engineers can explain why a decision was made and what tradeoffs it created.

I believe refactoring is a normal part of learning and improving.

I believe every project should leave me a better engineer than when I started.
