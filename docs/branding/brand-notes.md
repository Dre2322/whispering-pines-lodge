# Brand Notes

## Experience
A calm, intentional mountain retreat centered on connection.

## Primary Messages
- Escape the noise. Reconnect with the people who matter.
- Slow down. Disconnect. Reconnect.

## Visual Direction
Mountain mornings, pine forests, warm cabin light, natural textures, quiet family moments. Avoid party/resort-nightlife imagery.

## Voice
Warm, simple, calm, welcoming, and never overly luxurious or pretentious.
