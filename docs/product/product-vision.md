# Product Vision

## Company
Whispering Pines Hospitality

## Product
Whispering Pines Lodge

## Mission
To help people slow down, reconnect with nature, and create meaningful memories with the people who matter most through unforgettable mountain experiences.

## Brand Promise
Guests should feel like they have finally taken a deep breath—not simply checked into a hotel.

## Taglines
**Escape the noise. Reconnect with the people who matter.**

**Slow down. Disconnect. Reconnect.**

## Target Guests
- Couples
- Families
- Multi-generational families
- Small groups of friends
- Quiet spring-break travelers

## Not Our Audience
The lodge is not designed around nightlife, party culture, or large rowdy gatherings.

## Initial Accommodation Types
- Woodland Room
- Summit Suite
- Whispering Pines Cabin
